# Support

Toolkitty is free and open source (MIT). It was made by one person in their spare time.

## Ways to support it

- **Use it and tell someone.** The best support is a colleague who tries it. The `/about` pane copies a short "thanks" post with the repository link.
- **Star the repository.** It helps other people find it.
- **Report a bug or suggest an idea.** Open an issue at https://github.com/avraham-tech/toolkitty/issues. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- **A symbolic coffee.** If Toolkitty saved you time and you feel like it, a symbolic 1 to 5 dollars is warmly welcome at https://ko-fi.com/toolkitty or through GitHub Sponsors at https://github.com/sponsors/avraham-tech (pending approval).

## No obligation

Nothing in Toolkitty is locked, limited, or nagging. Every feature works the same whether or not you ever give anything. A single, quiet line in the weekly report mentions the coffee link after you have used Toolkitty for a while; press Dismiss and it never comes back.

## Questions

Type `/toolkitty help <question>` in Claude Code, or open an issue on the repository.
